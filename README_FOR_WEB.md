## What is autoclaw?

autoclaw is a 24/7 crew of AI agents that run continuously, building knowledge and improving our webpages. Where most AI systems fire once and forget, autoclaw accumulates.

## The crew

- **🔍 Researcher** — scans our 100+ webpages, finds gaps, writes new topics
- **📚 Teacher** — generates Q&A about every concept (5 levels deep)
- **⚖️ Critic** — validates every prose piece, scores confidence, flags contradictions
- **🧠 Distiller** — batches entries into canon pieces, exports to live-canon
